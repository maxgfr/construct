# Vision

**Product:** Readpile

## Problem
People save articles to read later but never find them again; hosted services lock data in and die.

## Target users
- self-hosting individuals
- small teams sharing a knowledge base

## Value proposition
Own your reading queue: a private, fast, searchable archive of everything you save.

## Success metrics
- A user re-finds any saved article in under 10 seconds, measured end to end from opening the app
- 1,000 self-hosted installs reporting in during the first year
